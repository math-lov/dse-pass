寫在開始之前：由現在的位置追上去 Before you start: catch up from where you are

無論你之前因為甚麼原因在數學科落後了進度，只要你今天打開這個網站，就代表你已經踏出追上來最重要的一步。 Whatever kept you behind in Maths before, opening this site today already means you have taken the most important step towards catching up.

這裡沒有壓力There is no pressure here

沒有倒數計時，也沒有分數排名。進度只會儲存在你手上的裝置裡 —— 老師看不到，也沒有任何人會拿你的表現來比較。 There is no countdown and no ranking. Your progress is stored only on your own device — your teacher cannot see it, and nobody will compare your performance with anyone else's.

你不需要為過去落後的課堂焦慮。我們由最核心、最穩陣能拿分的 DSE 課題開始，一課一課把基礎重新搭好。 You do not need to feel anxious about the lessons you missed. We start from the most central DSE topics, the ones that reliably earn marks, and rebuild the foundations one worksheet at a time.

讓自學真正見效的三個習慣Three habits that make self-study work

單憑雙眼看著螢幕按選項，不會把數學變成你的應試實力。要讓這個網站發揮效果，請每次都這樣做： Clicking options while staring at the screen will not turn Maths into exam ability. To make this site work, do the following every single time:

1
先備好工具 桌上要有筆、足夠的草稿紙，以及考評局認可的計算機。 Get your tools ready A pen, plenty of rough paper and an HKEAA-approved calculator on the desk.
2
先在紙上寫，才去選答案 自己親手算過才按選項，切忌直接猜。錯了也不要緊 —— 但你必須看見自己是怎樣錯。 Work on paper first, then choose the answer Do the calculation yourself before clicking an option; never just guess. Getting it wrong is fine — but you must be able to see how you went wrong.
3
答對答錯都要讀解說 提交答案後，讀一遍解題步驟與「陷阱分析」，拿著草稿紙核對自己在哪一個符號、括號，或者英文指令上走偏。 Read the explanation whether you were right or wrong After submitting, read the steps and the "trap analysis" with your rough work beside you and check which sign, bracket or English instruction you slipped on.

看不懂解說？三步求助Still lost? Three steps to get help

反覆閱讀後依然想不通，是自學的必經過程 —— 這不代表你能力不足，只是碰到了一個未被拆解的盲點。遇到障礙時，照這三步走： Reaching something you cannot figure out, even after reading it several times, is a normal part of self-study. It does not mean you lack ability — it means you have hit one blind spot that has not been unpacked yet. When that happens, follow these three steps:

1
精準說出你卡在哪一行 不要只說「我不懂」。用筆在草稿紙上標記清楚：是看不懂題目的英文關鍵詞？完全不知從何入手？還是第一、二步明白，純粹不明白第三步為甚麼突然變號？ 能指出具體哪一行想不通，問題就已經解決了一半。 Say exactly which line you are stuck on Do not just say "I don't get it". Mark it on your rough paper: is it a keyword in the English question? Not knowing where to begin at all? Or understanding steps 1–2 but not why step 3 suddenly flips a sign? Being able to point at the exact line already solves half the problem.
2
問 AI 助教（ChatGPT／Gemini／Claude） 深夜或放學自學時，生成式人工智能是你最方便的免費助手。把題目與你的草稿給它，並照下面的句子發問： Ask an AI tutor (ChatGPT / Gemini / Claude) Late at night or studying alone after school, a generative AI is your most convenient free assistant. Give it the question and your working, and ask using the template below: 
我是香港 DSE 數學科考生（以英文作答）。

題目：（貼上題目，或上載截圖）

我親手算到：（寫出你算到哪一步，或貼上草稿相片）

網站解說中我不明白的是：（抄出令你想不通的那一行）

請用繁體中文解釋這一步背後的代數原理。
如果解釋完我仍然不明白，請用簡單數字（例如代入 x = 1 或 x = 0）示範一次運算過程，直到我完全想通為止。I am a Hong Kong DSE Mathematics candidate (answering in English).

The question: (paste the question, or upload a screenshot)

What I have worked out myself: (the step you reached, or a photo of your working)

The line in the explanation that I don't understand: (copy that exact line)

Please explain the algebra behind that line in clear, simple English.
If I still don't get it, work through the calculation once with simple numbers
(e.g. substitute x = 1 or x = 0) until it completely clicks.
3
截圖問老師 如果 AI 解釋了兩三次依然說不通，不要在原位打轉、耗費心力： Screenshot it and ask your teacher If the AI explanation still does not click after two or three tries, do not keep going round in circles and wearing yourself out:
  • 截圖題目與你看不懂的解說，用紅圈圈出卡住的那一步； Screenshot the question and the explanation, and circle the step you are stuck on in red;
  • 拍下你親手計算的草稿紙； Photograph the rough paper with your own working;
  • 用通訊軟件（WhatsApp／學校平台）發給數學老師，並附上一句具體提問： Send both to your Maths teacher by WhatsApp or the school platform, with one specific question:
    老師，我自學這題時做到紅圈這一步想不通，草稿已附上，請問這裡應該如何理解？ Sir/Miss, I got stuck at the step circled in red while studying this question on my own. My working is attached — how should I understand this step?
  • 也可以先把題目加入 弱點升級庫，翌日帶手機與草稿找老師或同學面談 —— 老師看見你連思考過程都準備好，一定很樂意替你徹底解開問題。 You can also add the question to the weak-spot list and bring your phone and rough paper to your teacher or a classmate the next day — when a teacher sees that you have even prepared your thinking process, they will be glad to clear it up completely.

然後，繼續前進Then keep moving forward

遇到難題時，先截圖存好，深呼吸，然後做下一題。數學是累積的過程 —— 只要你持續在紙上動筆，進步就必定會發生。 When you hit a hard question, screenshot it, take a breath, and go on to the next one. Maths is cumulative — as long as you keep writing on paper, progress is guaranteed.
